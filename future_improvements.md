# 🗺️ GuildBoard: Future Roadmap & Improvements

This document outlines the planned features and technical upgrades to evolve GuildBoard from a task manager into a legendary RPG-inspired productivity engine.

---

## 🛠️ Core Features
- **Edit Quest** — Allow leaders and creators to modify titles, descriptions, and deadlines for quests that haven't started yet.
- **Quest Priority** — Add a `priority` field (Low, Medium, High, Critical) visualized with color-coded badges and sorting.
- **Quest Threads** — A dedicated comment section within each quest modal for team collaboration and progress updates.
- **File Attachments** — Upload screenshots or documents as "Proof of Work" when submitting quests for review.

## 🎮 Gamification & Engagement
- **Achievements & Badges** — A system to unlock unique titles (e.g., "Speedster," "Flawless Knight," "Bug Hunter") based on performance streaks.
- **Guild XP Pool** — Accumulate total guild XP to unlock guild-wide perks or rank up against other guilds on a global leaderboard.
- **Weekly Challenges** — Auto-generated objectives (e.g., "Team Effort: Complete 5 quests collectively") with bonus XP rewards.
- **Avatar Customization** — Unlock cosmetic items for profile avatars as users level up.

## 📊 Management & Automation
- **Quest Templates** — Save recurring tasks (e.g., "Weekly Sync," "Code Review") as templates to spawn new quests instantly.
- **Bulk Operations** — Duplicate quests or assign a single objective to multiple members simultaneously.
- **Smart Reminders** — Automated Discord or Email alerts when a deadline is within 24 hours.
- **Overdue Tracker** — A specialized dashboard view highlighting stalled or expired quests.

## 📈 Advanced Analytics
- **Exportable Reports** — Generate and download CSV/PDF monthly performance summaries for payroll or performance reviews.
- **Growth Trends** — Visual line charts showing XP and productivity growth over 3, 6, and 12-month periods.
- **Efficiency Metrics** — Track "Time to Completion" vs "Estimated Deadline" to help team capacity planning.

## 🧠 Antigravity's Advanced Upgrade Concepts
*My personal recommendations for taking the platform to the "S-Tier" level:*

### 1. 🤖 AI Dungeon Master (AI-Driven Management)
- **Auto-XP Balancing**: Use an LLM to analyze quest descriptions and automatically suggest a fair `base_xp` based on complexity.
- **Quest Generation**: Ask the AI to "Generate a sprint's worth of backend quests" based on your project goals.
- **Smart Review Summaries**: AI can summarize all council reviews and comments into a single "Feedback Report" for the member.

### 2. 🔌 Integration Ecosystem
- **GitHub/GitLab Sync**: Automatically mark a quest as "In Progress" when a branch is created, and "In Review" when a PR is opened.
- **Slack/Linear/Jira Bridges**: Two-way sync so GuildBoard can act as the "RPG Layer" on top of corporate tools.
- **Calendar Integration**: Sync quest deadlines directly to Google/Outlook calendars.

### 3. ⚔️ Guild Wars (Inter-Guild Competitions)
- **Guild vs Guild (GvG)**: Set up friendly competitions between different teams for a week. The team with the highest "Efficiency Score" wins a digital trophy.
- **Public Showcases**: A public-facing "Hall of Fame" where guilds can show off their completed legendary projects.

### 4. 🎭 Dynamic Narrative
- **Quest Storytelling**: Add a "Flavor Text" generator that turns boring tasks like "Fix Login Bug" into "Exorcise the Ghost of the Authentication Gate."
- **Level-up Events**: Celebrate a user reaching Lv. 10, 50, or 100 with a guild-wide animation and a custom "Legendary Item" badge.
